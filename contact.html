FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast do you deliver?
About 60 minutes across the city on average. You can also pick an exact delivery window at checkout.
How much is delivery?
₴150 within the city. Delivery is free for orders over ₴2,000.
Can I see the bouquet before it's sent?
Yes! We always send a photo of the finished bouquet, and you can ask us to change anything.
How long will the bouquet last?
Depending on the flowers, 7 to 14 days. Every bouquet comes with a care card.
Can I order anonymously?
Of course. Just enter the recipient's name and we'll keep the secret — the courier won't name the sender.
