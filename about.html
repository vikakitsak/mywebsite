We are FLORIS

A flower studio that believes a bouquet is more than flowers — it's a way to say what words can't.

Our story

Born from a love of velvet petals

FLORIS started with a simple idea: flowers should feel as special as the moment they're for. Deep wine tones, soft textures and careful hands — that's the mood behind every bouquet we make.

Today we arrange bouquets for birthdays, dates, weddings and quiet “just because” days, always fresh and always with a photo before delivery.
