Our story

Lukki was born as an answer — to overconsumption, to mass-market, to clothes made without a soul. We are curators, not a warehouse. We pick brands that think like us: transparent, ethical, honest. Every piece has a story. Every purchase leaves a trace. Fashion can be beautiful and kind.
